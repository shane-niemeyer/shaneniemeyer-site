Training is the process of health.

Training is not “working out.” It is structured and directed toward outcomes. It is progressive in nature, and periodized to develop strength and fitness, which are among the strongest predictors of health and longevity.

See how we work Work with Shane
“A healthy man wants a thousand things. A sick man wants only one.”
Proverb

Health and wellbeing is a process, and fitness is the absolute bedrock of that process. We reclaim lost capability and develop latent potential. This is the work of transformation. Truly. This work changes who we are, largely by changing what we are capable of.

Whatever your age. Wherever you are.

Set it down.

What you fear. What you think you can or can't do. Who you've been told you are.

The past matters only insofar as it informs where we begin. It tells us where you're starting. It doesn't tell us who you are.

The only thing that matters is now, and what's next.

This is the story of your life, and it's being written right now. You're the author. You can edit it. You can develop the main character. You can cut the old stories that aren't serving the plot.

So let's start with who you could become. How do you want to feel? How do you want your body to look, feel and move? What do you want your life to look like a year from now? Let your imagination run. That picture is what pulls you forward.

Then we train toward it.

In her 80s

From fearful to unafraid

  1. Day oneProne to falls. Really poor balance. Very unfit, aerobically and metabolically.
  2. 6 monthsDoing much better.

The doctrine

What we believe about training

Training is a way of being

Not a program we finish. A practice built into the fabric of daily life.

Reclaim, then develop

Gain access to as much of our physical capability as possible, at every age.

As much aerobic fitness as life allows

Combined training, weighted toward the aerobic side by time. Strength is never optional.

Meet people where they are

If we are healthy, we are training. If we are training, we are transforming.

Periodized, with rest built in

Fitness shows up during recovery, not during the work itself.

Body and mind are one system

As capability grows, so do confidence, resilience and our sense of what is possible.
