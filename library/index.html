The spine of the book

Two cities.

The fit cell and the sedentary cell, side by side.

The cell is a city: power plants (mitochondria), factories (ribosomes), roads and supply lines (capillaries, GLUT4). Cells aggregate into tissue, tissue into organs, organs into the human being. Each disease chapter opens by asking: what happened to the city?
