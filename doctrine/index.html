The doctrine

Training is not something we do a few hours a week.

It is the process of health and wellbeing itself, built into the fabric of daily life. Everything we publish, teach and coach flows from these principles.

Training is a way of being

Training is not a program we finish or a phase we pass through. It is a practice we build into the fabric of our lives and our daily process. The goal is not to complete training. The goal is to become someone who trains, for life.

Training is how we realize our human potential

Strength and power, muscular endurance, aerobic capacity, fundamental motor skills, every range and plane of motion, balance, body awareness, and the ability to get down to the ground and back up with ease. Each one we develop opens up more of life.

Aerobic fitness: as much as your life allows

The ideal is combined training, with the aerobic side carrying more of the weight by time and strength never optional. Cardiorespiratory fitness is one of the strongest predictors of how long and how well we live.

Meet people where they are

There is a path. It is everywhere and always, and it heads in the same direction for all of us. Where we stand on it is different for each person. Sometimes it is obscured by the weather of life, by circumstance, but like the sun, which is always shining above the clouds, it's there. Our work is to find where you are on it, and to travel it.

We start every person at their current level of capability and progress them rationally and methodically. Muscles adapt in weeks; tendons, ligaments, cartilage and bone adapt over months and years. Intelligent progression is what makes lifelong training possible.

Dynamic, periodized and built for recovery

Adaptation happens during recovery. Planned variation in volume and intensity, deliberate easier weeks, and room for life's disruptions keep progress moving for years instead of months. Rest is part of training.

Body and mind are one system

Our psychological states and our physical states shape each other. Training is one of the few truly transformative processes available in the human experience. It changes not just what we can do, but who we are.

See the target

We cannot hit a target we cannot see. We define, in as much detail as possible, how you want your body to look, feel and function, and then step into that picture. The clearer and more felt the vision, the more naturally the daily work follows.

No hacks. No shortcuts.

Some strategies simply work better than others, and we use the ones the evidence supports. The rest is the work itself, and the work is what transforms us.

Non-negotiables

What we never compromise on

  • Training is a lifelong practice, not a program.
  • Build as much aerobic fitness as life allows, always combined with strength.
  • Start where the person is, and progress at the pace of their slowest-adapting tissues.
  • Stay healthy first. Health keeps us training, and training transforms us.
  • Periodize, and build rest in by design.
  • Train the whole person: body and mind move together.
  • Make the target vivid, then show up consistently.
